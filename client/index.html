What is AutoFantasyTrade?

AutoFantasyTrade finds fair trades in your Sleeper or ESPN fantasy football league. You open a league, choose the players you want, and the site suggests 2-team and 3-team trades that fit each roster's needs.

How it works

  1. Open a Sleeper league with a username, or an ESPN league with its league id.
  2. Pick the players you want, and tap Keep on anyone who should stay off the trade block.
  3. Review trade ideas that stay within your fairness tolerance and fill positional needs.

Questions

Which leagues does AutoFantasyTrade support?

AutoFantasyTrade works with Sleeper and ESPN fantasy football leagues. On Sleeper, enter a username and choose a league. On ESPN, enter a league id. Public ESPN leagues load directly. Private ones also need the espn_s2 and SWID cookies from a signed-in ESPN session. Redraft, keeper, and dynasty leagues are supported, including 1QB and superflex.

Where do player values come from?

Player values come from Sleeper ADP or Yahoo's overall rank. Each rank is converted into a trade value in the app. Those rankings are saved as snapshots, so a search does not request them again every time.

What makes a trade fair?

A trade is fair when every team stays inside the fairness tolerance you set. The comparison adds a premium when one side consolidates several players into a single star, so one high-value player can outweigh a package of depth pieces. You can widen or tighten that tolerance before you search.

How do 3-team trades work?

A 3-team trade brings in a third roster when two teams alone cannot make a fair deal that also fills positional needs. You still receive the players you targeted. The third team sends players where a roster is thin and receives players where it has a surplus.

Can I keep players off the trade block?

Yes. On your roster, tap Keep next to any player you do not want to trade. Kept players still count toward your lineup and positional needs, and the search will not offer them. Tap Kept to put a player back on the trade block.

Is AutoFantasyTrade free, and do I need an account?

AutoFantasyTrade is free, and you do not create an account. A Sleeper league needs a username. An ESPN league needs its league id, and a private ESPN league also needs your espn_s2 and SWID cookies.
